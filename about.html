People have always been the point.

I'm Hannah, a content creator and social media storyteller based in Hammond, Indiana. I love finding the human side of a product or idea and turning it into content that feels natural, engaging, and easy to connect with.

My experience in customer-facing roles taught me how to listen, communicate clearly, and understand what makes people feel seen. I bring that same care to every concept: thinking about the audience, finding the strongest angle, and making the message feel like a real conversation.

How I like to create

I believe good content can be polished without feeling overproduced. A strong opening, an honest point of view, and a little personality can go a long way. Whether I'm sharing a product experience, speaking directly to camera, or exploring a trend, I want the result to feel true to the brand and relatable to the person watching.

  • Story first
    Find the angle that makes the message meaningful.
  • Audience-aware
    Make content feel native to the platform and natural to watch.
  • Collaborative mindset
    Clear communication, thoughtful ideas, and a focus on the brief.
  • Always curious
    Learning from trends, people, and the details others might miss.

What I can bring to a brand

UGC videos, product storytelling, lifestyle content, talking-to-camera videos, trend-led concepts, and a warm, relatable on-screen presence. My goal is to help brands build trust by showing up in a way that feels human.
